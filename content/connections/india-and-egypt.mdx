## A Mauryan message to Egypt

Ashoka's Edict XIII lists five Greek kings, including Ptolemy of Egypt, as rulers to whom his
message of dhamma had been sent <Cite id="hultzsch-1925" />. It is one of the earliest named links
between India and Egypt, though we only have the Indian side of the story.

## The Red Sea route

After the Romans took Egypt in 30 BCE, trade with India grew quickly. Ships left the Red Sea
ports in summer and used the monsoon winds to cross the Arabian Sea <Cite id="casson-1989" />
<Cite id="mclaughlin-2010" />. At Berenike, archaeologists have found the goods that made the
journey worthwhile, from Indian pepper to teak used in local buildings
<Cite id="sidebotham-2011" />.
