## How we know

No Indus text describes this trade, because the Indus script cannot be read. The evidence
comes from objects and from Mesopotamian writing. Indus seals, weights and beads have been
found in Mesopotamia and the Gulf, and Mesopotamian texts name a land called Meluhha that
sent ships, timber and precious stones <Cite id="ratnagar-2004" /> <Cite id="possehl-2002" />.

## What it tells us about the Indus cities

The trade shows that Indus craft workers made goods valued far away, such as long carnelian
beads drilled with great skill. It also shows that Indus merchants used standard weights,
which suggests well-organised exchange even without visible kings <Cite id="kenoyer-1998" />.
