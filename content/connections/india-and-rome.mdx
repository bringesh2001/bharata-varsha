## What moved, and why

India had what Roman buyers wanted: black pepper from the Malabar coast, fine cotton cloth,
pearls and precious stones. The Periplus, written by a merchant for merchants, lists these goods
port by port <Cite id="casson-1989" />. In return, Roman traders brought gold and silver coins,
wine, glass and coral <Cite id="mclaughlin-2010" />.

## The evidence on the ground

Hoards of Roman coins have been found in South India, and Roman pottery has been excavated at
coastal sites. At Arikamedu, archaeologists found Roman wine jars and fine tableware
<Cite id="wheeler-1946" />. Tamil Sangam poetry also mentions *Yavana* (foreign) ships arriving
with gold and leaving with pepper <Cite id="mclaughlin-2010" />.
