## Where the claim came from

In *The History of British India* (1817), James Mill dismissed Indian civilisation as backward
and without history, a view that helped justify British rule <Cite id="mill-1817" />.

## A Kashmiri chronicle

Kalhana's *Rajatarangini*, written in the twelfth century, tells the history of Kashmir's kings in
verse. Kalhana says he consulted older works and inscriptions, and he reflects on how a historian
should judge the past <Cite id="stein-1900" />.

## Many forms of history

Romila Thapar argues that early India had several "historical traditions", from the genealogies
of the Puranas to inscriptions and biographies, each recording the past for a different purpose
<Cite id="thapar-2013" /> <Cite id="thapar-2002" />.
