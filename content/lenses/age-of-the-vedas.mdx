## Why it is hard

The Vedas were composed and handed down orally, with remarkable precision, for many centuries
before being written. The text itself rarely refers to datable events, so dates have to be
argued from language, from what the hymns mention, and from links to archaeology
<Cite id="jamison-brereton-2014" />.

## The epics are different

The Mahabharata and Ramayana are huge texts that grew over time. Scholars generally see a core
story expanded by later additions over several centuries, so asking "how old is the
Mahabharata" has more than one answer <Cite id="brockington-1998" />.

## Connected debates

The dating question is tied to the debate about [where the Vedic people came
from](/lenses/vedic-origins). An early date usually goes with the view that Vedic culture began
inside India <Cite id="bryant-2001" />.
